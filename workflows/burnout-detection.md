## What It Does

Every Sunday morning, this workflow analyzes your past 7 days of mood, sleep, energy, and task-completion data. It looks for the patterns you're too deep in the fog to notice yourself:

- **3+ days of declining mood** → Early alert
- **Sleep dropping below 6 hours** → Warning triggered
- **Task completion falling while mood appears "fine"** → Hidden burnout detection
- **Missed meals or skipped eating** → Critical intervention

When it detects a pre-crash trajectory, it sends you a Telegram message that's warm, direct, and concrete — not a clinical alert. "Your patterns match what happened before your last crash. Scale back for 48 hours. Eat, sleep, hydrate. Everything else can wait."

## Why It Matters

This is the workflow I built for myself after losing 3-week blocks to depressive crashes I didn't see coming. Standard mood trackers show you the data. This one *interprets* it and warns you in time to act.

With Bipolar disorder, the difference between catching a downswing early and missing it is measured in weeks of your life. This workflow is the safety net.
