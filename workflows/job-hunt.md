## What It Does

Job hunting with ADHD or during a depressive phase is brutal. Opening job boards leads to overwhelm. Customizing resumes for each role is exhausting. Most people burn out within 2-3 days.

This workflow changes the dynamic:

1. You tell it your target role, industry, and seniority — once
2. It searches for relevant jobs (3-5 at a time, not 50)
3. For each job, it generates 3-4 tailored resume bullets + a 2-sentence cover note
4. You get copy-paste-ready output — no rewriting, no starting from scratch
5. It stops you after 3 applications: "Good work. Pick this up tomorrow. Applications are better when you're fresh."

## Why It Matters

The traditional job hunt workflow is: search → get overwhelmed → procrastinate → self-blame → repeat. This workflow turns it into: search → pick one → get a prepared application → submit → done. Small batches, no burnout.

I built this after two years without a job, knowing that the application process itself was the barrier — not my qualifications.
