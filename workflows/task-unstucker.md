## What It Does

You know that feeling — you need to deploy, or write that email, or start that project, but you're frozen. Not lazy. Not procrastinating. Your brain just won't let you take the first step.

Tell the agent "stuck" or "I can't start," and it:

1. Asks what you're stuck on (accepts any answer — even "everything")
2. Breaks the task into exactly 5 micro-steps, each under 5 minutes
3. Makes step 1 absurdly easy — "Open terminal" or "Stand up"
4. Waits for you to say "done" before giving you step 2
5. If you get stuck on a step, breaks THAT step into even smaller pieces

No pep talks. No motivation quotes. Just a ladder out of the paralysis pit.

## Why It Matters

Task paralysis isn't about willpower — it's an executive function wall. ADHD and depression both attack the brain's ability to sequence actions. Breaking "deploy the project" into "1. Open terminal, 2. cd into folder, 3. git status" bypasses the part of your brain that's frozen.

This is the workflow I use multiple times a week. It's the difference between a lost day and a done task.
